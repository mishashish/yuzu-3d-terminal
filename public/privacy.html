$YUZU CRYPTO COMPANION // CHROME EXTENSION

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Data Collection

$YUZU Crypto Companion does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, wallet private keys, or user analytics.

2. Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) exclusively on your device to save:

  • Your widget visibility preference (widgetEnabled).
  • Your price movement alert toggle (alertsEnabled) and alert cooldown timestamp.
  • Cached public cryptocurrency prices (SOL, BTC, ETH, WIF, BONK, PEPE, JUP) for fast popup display.

3. Network Requests

To display live 24-hour market data and render the embedded 3D companion inside the popup, the extension connects only to:

  • Public market price APIs: okx.com, api.binance.com, and api.coingecko.com.
  • The official 3D mini-stage hosted at https://yuzuha-simulator.vercel.app/?mini=1.

No authentication tokens, cookies, or personal user data are sent to any third-party service.

4. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Saves user widget/alert settings and cached market data locally.
  • alarms: Periodically refreshes public crypto prices every 2 minutes in the background.
  • notifications: Sends optional desktop alerts when a tracked coin moves by 5% or more in 24 hours.

5. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue on our official repository: github.com/mishashish/yuzu-3d-terminal.
